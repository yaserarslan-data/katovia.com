CREATE A CHALLENGE
A little challenge together

Create a short, casual challenge with Reaction Duel. Press READY, then tap on the TAP signal after a random delay. An early tap lets you retry without recording a score. Send the result link to a friend; it carries your target reaction time. A lower time wins, a higher time loses, and equal times tie. After the result, rematch or share a new link with your own score. Scores are measured in the browser, targets can be edited, and this is not a verified ranking. It is independent of your Daily score. Leaving the tab interrupts an active attempt.
