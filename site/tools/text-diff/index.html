Text Diff Checker

Compare two texts line by line and see added and removed lines.

Enter text in Before and After. A minus marks a removed line, a plus marks an added line, and shared lines begin with two spaces. The tool uses a longest common subsequence algorithm; each text may contain at most 200 lines. Compare smaller sections of large files. Whitespace and letter case matter. Copy the output when ready. This tool does not provide character-level differences or merge files. Your text stays on your device.
