PARTICLE UNIVERSE

A tiny universe made of light. Touch, attract, scatter and shape your own orbit.

Use mouse, touch or keyboard.

Particle Universe is a light-and-motion experiment generated in your browser. Attract gathers particles around the focus, repel pushes them away, and orbit adds a circulating flow. Clicking or tapping creates a short burst. Switch modes and pause the motion to compare what happens. You can capture the scene using your own screen recording. Visuals are produced by original code; no download, login or external media is needed. Quality adapts to the display and frame time. A reduced-motion preference starts the experience paused.
