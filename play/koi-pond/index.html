KOI POND

Take a moment. Touch the water, feed the fish and explore a calm pond.

Use mouse, touch or keyboard.

Koi Pond is a small digital garden drawn with original Canvas code. Fish drift calmly; touching the water sends out ripples and drops food. Fish approach the nearest food, then return to wandering. Tap on your phone, use a mouse on desktop, or move the focus with a keyboard. Pause the animation for a quieter moment. No external image, music or sound file is loaded. With reduced motion enabled, the pond starts still; resume whenever you want to explore.
