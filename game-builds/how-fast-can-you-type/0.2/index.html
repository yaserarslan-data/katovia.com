How to play +

Type the displayed text, including spaces, punctuation, and line breaks (↵ = Enter). Correct characters turn mint; mistakes turn coral. Backspace and selection edits are allowed. Pasting and dropping text are disabled.

Reach the end before time runs out with at least 90% of the final text matching. Earn ★ for a clear, ★★ at 95% accuracy, or ★★★ at 98%. Stars use the lower of typing accuracy and final text accuracy.

WPM counts currently correct characters ÷ 5 per minute. Typing accuracy counts correct inserted characters across all attempts; fixing a mistake does not erase it. Pausing or switching tabs freezes the timer. Records stay in this browser.
