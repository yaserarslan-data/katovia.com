FACILITY 09 / FIELD GUIDE

TRUST YOUR MEMORY.

Use the Surveillance Wall to scan all available cameras. Click a feed to inspect it in Focus View. Choose a camera and category, then file a report. Correct reports restore the room and reduce instability by 14 points.

LIGHTING ANOMALY / NIGHT 3
The whole room has fallen into emergency lighting. Normal darkness alone is not an incident.
CAMERA INTERFERENCE / NIGHT 4
Persistent broad tracking bands and a damaged picture. Normal fine CRT grain is not an incident.

Open incidents steadily raise Building Instability. Overdue incidents stay visible and raise it faster. Later nights allow more concurrent incidents and a longer reporting window. Check the duty roster before each watch.

100% instability or three false reports ends the night. Survive the full watch to reach 03:00. Nights last 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 active minutes.
