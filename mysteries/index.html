TopicCore questionBrief assessmentStatus
Shroud of TurinRead the caseHow did the body image form, and is the cloth really associated with Jesus?The precise image-forming mechanism is disputed. The claim that modern technology cannot reproduce it is overstated; substantial evidence supports a medieval date.🟡 Partly explained
Voynich ManuscriptRead the caseWhich language or encoding system produced this text?The physical manuscript and language-like patterns are known; a verified reading remains unavailable.🔴 Core question open
Antikythera MechanismRead the caseWhat were its complete design and manufacturing origins?Its astronomical functions are well understood; missing components and origins leave open questions.🟡 Partly explained
Nazca LinesRead the caseWhy were these enormous figures made?An alien-runway explanation is unsupported. Rituals, walking routes, sacred landscapes, social communication and possibly water/celestial connections may have played roles together.🟡 Partly explained
Codex GigasRead the caseWas it written in one night with the Devil’s help?The single-night account and pact with the Devil are legends. Much of the manuscript was probably the work of one scribe over many years.🟢 Main claim largely explained
Copper ScrollRead the caseDid the enormous listed treasure really exist, and where is it?The text resembles a genuine treasure inventory. The treasure has not been found, and a Temple-treasury origin is unproven.🔴 Core question open
Baigong PipesRead the caseWho made China’s supposed “ancient metal pipes”?There is no strong evidence that these are human-made pipes. Natural mineralisation, concretions and root casts offer a much better explanation.🟢 Main claim largely explained
Yonaguni MonumentRead the caseIs the underwater stepped formation natural or human-made?A natural geological origin for the main mass has stronger support. Ancient human use or partial modification cannot be entirely excluded.🟡 Partly explained
Saksaywaman / Puma PunkuRead the caseHow did two distinct cultures build these stone structures?Claims of impossible technology are not the main explanation; the technical details of each site need separate assessment.🟢 Main claim largely explained
Roman DodecahedraRead the caseWhat were these twelve-sided bronze objects used for?Proposals include measurement, knitting, candleholders, military tools, games and ritual. None has been established.🔴 Core question open
Rongorongo ScriptRead the caseWhat do Rapa Nui’s signs say?There is strong support for an organised writing/notation system. A lunar calendar has probably been recognised on Mamari; the remaining texts cannot be read.🔴 Core question open
Phaistos DiscRead the caseWhich language does the spiral script encode?A structured system, probably syllabic. With only one principal document, proposed decipherments are almost impossible to verify.🔴 Core question open
Baghdad BatteryRead the caseDid ancient Mesopotamians use electricity?The arrangement can generate electricity, but no archaeological technological chain demonstrates ancient use as a battery.🟡 Partly explained
Dendera LightRead the caseDid Egyptians depict an electric light bulb?The strongest explanation is religious iconography of Harsomtus’s birth from the lotus. There is no independent evidence of electrical technology.🟢 Main claim largely explained
